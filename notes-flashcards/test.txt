Linux is an open-source operating system kernel created by Linus Torvalds in 1991. It forms the foundation of distributions like Ubuntu, Fedora, and Debian.
TCP is a connection-oriented protocol that ensures reliable delivery of data packets across IP networks using handshakes and acknowledgments.
IAM stands for Identity and Access Management, which is a framework of policies and technologies for ensuring that the proper people in an enterprise have the appropriate access to technology resources.
